Keisuke Sakaguchi is a professor at Tohoku University and a research scientist at the RIKEN Center for Advanced Intelligence Project. His research lies at the intersection of natural language processing, machine learning, and psycholinguistics. His long-term goals are to understand the principles of human intelligence and develop robust and efficient AI systems capable of learning, reasoning, and interacting with the real world. His work spans the development, analysis, and evaluation of language and multimodal AI systems. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University and was previously a research scientist at the Allen Institute for AI (AI2). His papers have received Outstanding Paper Awards at AAAI 2020 and ACL 2017.
